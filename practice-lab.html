Works best in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer. Browsers’ speech engines often delete “um” and “uh” from the text, so Filler Buster also listens for the sound of a held “ummm” or “uhhh”. That part is new and approximate. Long stretched words like “sooo” may count too, because they are hesitations as well. Your voice is processed by your browser’s speech service (for example, Google in Chrome). SLW Academy does not record or save anything.
